Quick Reads

Understanding Burnout

Burnout is a state of physical or emotional exhaustion that also involves a sense of reduced accomplishment and loss of personal identity. It's not just "being tired." It often feels like you have nothing left to give and you may feel cynical about your work or studies. Common signs include chronic fatigue, feeling unmotivated, and detaching from your responsibilities.

The 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding Technique

When you feel overwhelmed, use this technique to ground yourself. Look around and name:
5 things you can SEE.
4 things you can FEEL.
3 things you can HEAR.
2 things you can SMELL.
1 thing you can TASTE.
This simple exercise pulls your mind away from anxious thoughts and anchors it in the present moment.
